How It Works

Verify every customer call in seconds

Your agents use a web portal or API. Your customers use the free SimplyAuth app.

1

Get verified

Your organization goes through our verification process. You receive a verified badge that customers can trust and that can't be impersonated.

2

Invite your customers

Send customers an invite to add your company as a trusted connection in SimplyAuth. It's free for them — business connections never count against their plan.

3

Agent triggers verification

When your agent calls a customer, they trigger a verification from the web portal or via API. A unique, cryptographically random phrase appears on both sides.

4

Customer confirms

The customer sees your company name with a verified badge and the matching phrase. If it matches what the agent reads — it's really you. If not, they hang up.

Impersonation scams targeting financial services are the most costly. Let customers verify that a fraud alert call is genuine before taking action on their account.

Insurance

Claims adjusters calling policyholders about sensitive claims. Verify the call is from the real insurance company before discussing personal or medical details.

Tax agencies, social security offices, and government services are frequently impersonated. Give citizens a way to verify the call is legitimate.

Why SimplyAuth

What makes this different

Verified Badge

Your company gets a verified badge that no one can impersonate. Name protection prevents scammers from creating lookalike accounts.

Free for Your Customers

Business connections never count against your customers' plan. They download the app, connect with you, and verify — all free.

API Integration

Integrate with your call center software. Trigger verifications programmatically when agents dial out. Works with Twilio, Genesys, Five9, and more.

Cryptographically Secure

Every phrase is generated with a CSPRNG and expires in 60 seconds. No AI, no voice analysis, no biometrics — just math that can't be faked.

Web Portal for Agents

Your agents use a browser-based dashboard — no app install needed. Search customers, trigger verifications, track results.

Brand Protection
