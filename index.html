Built-in safety, too

Going somewhere alone? Set a check-in timer. If you don't tap "I'm OK" before it expires, your trusted connections get an automatic alert. A silent distress alert can also go to everyone you've verified, in one tap.

  • Preset or custom durations up to 24 hours
  • Add an optional note for context
  • Choose which connections get alerted
  • Extend by 30 minutes if you need more time
  • Send a silent distress alert to all your connections in one tap

We can't read what you send

Everything is encrypted before it leaves your phone, and the keys stay on your device. Our servers hold only encrypted data we cannot open.

No phone number, ever

You connect through an invite you share yourself. No one can look you up, and you'll never appear in anyone's contact suggestions.

No groups, no wrong room

SimplyAuth is 1:1 by design. There's no group to be added to, so the "wrong person in the room" failure can't happen.

We do keep connection records (who paired with whom, and when) so the app works. What we keep and why is listed on our privacy page. And to be clear about neighbors: Signal is built for private conversations. SimplyAuth is built for one job — confirming who you're talking to before you hand over something sensitive. Use both.

Why I built this

AI scams, hacking, and constant surveillance have taken something basic from us: knowing who's really on the other end, and being able to share something private without it living on a server forever. I wanted a tool where you check the person first, the sharing is encrypted, and nothing sticks around afterward. That's the whole idea.
