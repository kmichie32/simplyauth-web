Features

Everything you need to stay safe

SimplyAuth gives you and your trusted contacts a simple, fast way to verify identity and stay connected.

60-Second Verification

Cryptographically random phrases expire in 60 seconds. Read the phrase over the phone to prove you're really you. No way to fake it.

Trusted Connections

Pair with family, friends, or colleagues using QR codes or invite links. Only people you trust can verify with you.

Safety Check-Ins

Set a timer before heading out. If you don't check in by the deadline, your selected contacts are automatically alerted.

Distress Alerts

Set a secret panic word stored only on your device. If someone forces you to "verify," entering it silently alerts all your contacts.

Biometric App Lock

Protect the app with Face ID or fingerprint. Your verification data stays locked even if someone picks up your phone.

Instant Notifications

Get notified the moment a contact requests verification or if a check-in is missed. High-priority alerts for emergencies.

How It Works

Verify anyone in four steps

It takes less than 60 seconds to confirm someone is who they say they are.

1

Add a Connection

Scan a QR code or share an invite link to pair with someone you trust.

2

Get a Call

3

Exchange Phrases

A unique phrase appears on both devices. Read it aloud to confirm identity.

4

Verified

Phrases match? You're talking to the real person. Mismatch? Hang up.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Phone scams and impersonation are on the rise. Criminals use AI-generated voices, spoofed caller IDs, and social engineering to pretend to be someone you know. SimplyAuth lets you instantly verify that the person on the other end of a call is who they claim to be, using a time-limited phrase that only appears in your app and theirs.
Each phrase is cryptographically random (generated with a CSPRNG), unique to that moment, and expires in 60 seconds. It is never spoken by the app or transmitted over the phone line. Both parties must have the app open to see it. An attacker on the call cannot guess or intercept it.
The panic word is a secret word or phrase you choose that is stored only on your device and never sent to any server. If you're being coerced during a verification, entering your panic word instead of the real phrase silently sends a high-priority distress alert to all your trusted contacts without the attacker knowing.
Yes. The free plan includes all core features: identity verification, safety check-ins, distress alerts, and biometric app lock. You get 1 admin connection for free, which is enough for most personal use. The Personal plan ($1.99/month or $19.99/year) expands your limit to 10 admin connections for families or small teams.
When you create a connection (send the invite), you're the admin and it counts toward your plan limit. When someone else invites you, you're the receiver and it doesn't count against your limit. Receiver connections are always unlimited on every plan.
Only what's needed to make the app work: your name, email, profile photo (from Google/Apple sign-in), device tokens for push notifications, and connection/verification records. Verification phrases auto-delete within 24 hours. We never collect your panic word, phone number, location, or contacts. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
No. SimplyAuth is not an emergency service. The distress alert feature is designed to notify your trusted contacts on a best-effort basis. Notification delivery depends on third-party services and is not guaranteed. In any emergency, always call 911 (or your local emergency number) first.
