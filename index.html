Why SimplyAuth?

Simple, secure identity verification for the people you care about.

How It Works

Three steps to verify anyone's identity

1

Add a Connection

Share an invite code or scan a QR code to link with someone you trust. It takes seconds.

2

Get a suspicious call?

Tap "Verify" on your connection's card. A unique, time-limited phrase appears on both phones simultaneously.

3

Read the phrase aloud

If what they read matches your screen, it's really them. If not, hang up immediately.

Safety Feature

Scheduled Check-Ins

Going somewhere alone? Set a timer. If you don't tap "I'm OK" before it expires, your selected connections get an automatic alert.

  • Preset or custom durations up to 24 hours
