Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Data Controller

SimplyAuth LLC ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller responsible for your personal data. For any privacy-related inquiries, contact us at support@simplyauth.app.

What We Collect

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL. We also store a unique account identifier and device tokens used to deliver push notifications to your device.

When you connect with other users and perform verifications, we store connection records (who connected with whom) and verification records (who verified whom, when, and the outcome). Verification phrases are ephemeral — they expire after 60 seconds and are automatically deleted within 24 hours.

What We Never Collect

Your panic / distress word is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. We do not collect your phone number, location, contacts, or payment card details.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — To provide the SimplyAuth service you signed up for, including identity verification, connection management, and push notifications.
  • Legitimate interest — To maintain the security and integrity of the service, prevent abuse, and improve the app experience.
  • Consent — For optional features such as promotional communications. You may withdraw consent at any time.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide real-time identity verification between you and your trusted contacts
  • To send push notifications when a verification request is directed to you
  • To deliver emergency, distress, and check-in alerts to your trusted contacts
  • To maintain your list of trusted connections
  • To display your verification history
  • To manage your subscription (Personal plan)

Third-Party Services

Google Firebase (Alphabet Inc.) — Authentication, real-time database, push notifications, and cloud functions. Firebase processes data on servers located in the United States. Firebase Privacy Policy

RevenueCat Inc. — Subscription and in-app purchase management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Apple Inc. / Google LLC — Sign-in and payment processing via their respective platforms.

Cross-Border Data Transfers

SimplyAuth uses Google Firebase, which stores and processes data on servers located in the United States. If you are located outside the United States — including in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, India, South Africa, or elsewhere — your personal data will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

These transfers are protected by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and GDPR commitments, which provide safeguards for the protection of your data in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Data Retention

Verification records are automatically deleted within 24 hours. Your account, connection, and subscription data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us — we will remove your account and all associated records within 30 days.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"). You can also delete your account directly from the Profile screen in the app.
  • Data portability — Request your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction — Request that we limit the processing of your data.
  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent — Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@simplyauth.app. We will respond within 30 days (or sooner where required by local law). If you are in the EEA or UK and believe your rights have not been addressed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

International Users

SimplyAuth is available in multiple countries. We are committed to complying with applicable data protection laws in each jurisdiction, including:

  • EEA & UK — General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR
  • India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
  • South Africa — Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)
  • Kenya — Data Protection Act, 2019
  • Nigeria — Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)
  • Singapore — Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)
  • Philippines — Data Privacy Act of 2012
  • Malaysia — Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)
  • UAE — Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection

Regardless of your location, you may exercise the data rights listed above by contacting us. We apply the same high standard of data protection to all users worldwide.

Security

All data is transmitted using TLS encryption and protected by Firebase Security Rules, which ensure that only you and your verified connections can access shared data. Verification phrases are never transmitted over the phone — they exist only inside the app and expire automatically.

Children

SimplyAuth is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from children under these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
