Description of Service

SimplyAuth provides a real-time identity verification tool that allows trusted contacts to confirm each other's identity over a phone call using time-limited, cryptographically random verification phrases. The App also includes safety features such as scheduled check-ins, emergency alerts, and distress signals. The service is designed to help protect against phone scams, social engineering, and to provide peace of mind to you and your trusted contacts.
