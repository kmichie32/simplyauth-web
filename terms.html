Description of Service

SimplyAuth is a secure messaging app built around Verified Sessions — short, end-to-end encrypted conversations for sharing sensitive information such as passwords, account numbers, and other private details that would otherwise be sent over insecure channels. Messages are encrypted on your device, and our servers store only ciphertext (we cannot read your messages). A session is limited to two people, ends after a fixed time and message limit, and its contents are automatically wiped from both devices when the room closes. No phone number is required to use Verified Sessions.

Underpinning the service is a real-time identity verification tool that lets trusted contacts confirm each other's identity over a phone call using time-limited, cryptographically random verification phrases, helping protect against phone scams and social engineering. SimplyAuth also includes safety features — scheduled check-ins and silent duress / SOS alerts to trusted contacts.
